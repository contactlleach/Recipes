Zesty Italian Lentil & Cannellini Warm Salad

Prep: 5 mins
Cook: 7 mins

✓ Prep Ahead
✓ High Fibre

Shopping List

  • Canned Green Lentils & Cannellini Beans
  • Tenderstem Broccoli or Baby Spinach
  • Sun-Dried Tomatoes (chopped)
  • Fresh Lemon, Garlic & Olive Oil
  • 0% Greek Yoghurt & Dijon Mustard

Cooking Instructions

1
Char Greens: Dry-pan char trimmed broccoli in a hot skillet for 4–5 mins until crisp-tender and lightly browned. Remove and set aside.
2
Warm Pulses: Heat olive oil in skillet. Add drained lentils, cannellini beans, and chopped sun-dried tomatoes. Warm through over medium heat for 3 mins.
3
Whisk Dressing: In a small bowl, whisk together Greek yoghurt, Dijon mustard, fresh lemon juice, and zest with salt and pepper.
4
Assemble & Serve: Toss charred broccoli with warm pulses. Drizzle creamy lemon-Dijon dressing across bowls and serve warm.
