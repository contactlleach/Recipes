Shopping List

  • 0% Greek-Style Natural Yogurt
  • Fresh or Frozen Mixed Berries
  • Whole Chia Seeds or Flaxseeds
  • Raw Walnuts (chopped)

Preparation Instructions

1
Base: Portion the thick 0% Greek yogurt into your serving bowl.
2
Berries: Top with fresh berries (or warm frozen berries in the microwave for 30–45 seconds until juicy).
3
Fibre & Crunch: Scatter the chia seeds (or flaxseeds) and chopped walnuts evenly across the bowl.
4
Serve: Enjoy immediately, or seal in an airtight glass jar for up to 24 hours in the fridge.
