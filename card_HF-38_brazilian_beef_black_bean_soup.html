Speedy Brazilian Style Beef & Black Bean Soup

Cook: 10 mins

Cooking Instructions

1
Toast Ciabatta: Preheat oven to 200°C. Slice ciabatta into finger-sized dippers, place on a baking tray, drizzle with olive oil, and bake for 6–8 mins until crisp and golden.
2
Brown Beef & Pepper: Heat oil in a large saucepan over high heat. Add diced bell pepper and beef mince; fry for 4–5 mins, breaking up with a spoon until nicely browned.
3
Build Feijoada Stew: Stir in Brazilian spice mix, red wine stock paste, passata, drained black beans, and 200ml boiling water per 2 portions. Simmer briskly for 5 mins until slightly thickened.
4
Wilt Spinach & Serve: Take off heat and fold in baby spinach until wilted. Ladle hearty stew into deep bowls and serve with toasted ciabatta dippers for mopping up sauce.
