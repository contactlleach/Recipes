Super Quick Chorizo & Bean Stew with Toasts

Prep: 5 mins
Cook: 10 mins

✓ Freeze Stew

✓ Calorie Smart

Shopping List

  • Diced Chorizo & Mixed Beans (tinned)
  • Soffritto Mix & Bell Pepper
  • Tomato Passata & Smoked Paprika
  • Red Wine Stock Paste
  • Ciabatta Rolls
  • Grated Hard Italian Style Cheese

Cooking Instructions

1
Toast Ciabatta: Slice ciabatta diagonally into toasts. Drizzle with a little olive oil, place on a baking sheet, and bake at 200°C for 5–7 mins until crunchy.
2
Fry Chorizo & Soffritto: Heat a dry pan over medium heat. Sauté diced chorizo for 2–3 mins until oils release. Add soffritto mix, diced pepper, and smoked paprika; fry for 3–4 mins until softened.
3
Simmer Stew: Stir in drained mixed beans, passata, red wine stock paste, and 50ml water per 2 portions. Simmer gently for 5 mins until the stew is rich and bubbly.
4
Garnish & Serve: Spoon thick stew into bowls, scatter generously with grated hard Italian style cheese, and serve with warm ciabatta toasts for dipping.
