Cooking Instructions

1
Roast Veg: Toss cubed sweet potato and sliced red onion with oil, thyme, salt, and pepper. Roast at 200°C for 18–20 mins until tender.
2
Prepare Couscous: Place couscous in a bowl with 120ml boiling water (for 2 portions) and salt. Cover for 5 mins, then fluff with a fork.
3
Whisk Dressing: Emulsify olive oil, soy sauce, honey, and red wine vinegar with a pinch of black pepper.
4
Assemble: Combine couscous, diced beetroot, roasted sweet potato/onion, and rocket. Drizzle with dressing, crumble goat's cheese, and top with crispy onions.
