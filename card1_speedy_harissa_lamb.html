Cooking Instructions

1
Cook Bulgur: Cook bulgur wheat in boiling seasoned stock water (approx. 10–12 mins) until tender, then drain and fluff with a fork.
2
Brown Lamb & Veg: Sauté sliced pepper and lamb mince in a hot pan over medium-high heat until browned; drain off any excess fat.
3
Simmer Sauce: Stir in minced garlic, harissa, stock paste, passata, and a dash of water. Simmer for 4–5 mins until rich and thick.
4
Finish & Plate: Fold in baby spinach until wilted. Plate next to fluffy bulgur wheat and top with a dollop of Greek yoghurt.
