Tuscan Sun-Dried Tomato & Herb Tuna Skillet

Prep: 5 mins
Cook: 5 mins
✓ High Protein
✗ Don't Freeze
✓ Fridge 3 Days
✓ Glucose Friendly

Shopping List

  • Canned Tuna in Spring Water
  • Cannellini Beans (carton or tin)
  • Jarred Roasted Red Peppers
  • Sun-Dried Tomato Pesto
  • Dried Oregano, Smoked Paprika, Parsley

Cooking Instructions

1
Warm the Base: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Sauté sliced peppers, paprika, and oregano for 2 mins until fragrant.
2
Layer Flavour: Stir in sun-dried tomato pesto and 2 tbsp water or stock to create a glossy sauce.
3
Simmer Beans: Tip in rinsed cannellini beans. Stir to coat and cook for 3–4 mins until hot throughout.
4
Fold Tuna & Serve: Remove from heat, gently fold in drained tuna in large flakes, and cover for 1 min to warm through. Finish with parsley and black pepper.
