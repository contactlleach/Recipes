Whipped Berry & Nut Butter Protein Bowl

Shopping List

  • 0% Greek-Style Natural Yoghurt
  • Unflavoured Whey or Plant Protein
  • Frozen Mixed Berries
  • Smooth Peanut or Almond Butter
  • Whole Chia Seeds

Preparation Instructions

1
Whip Protein Base: In a mixing bowl, vigorously whisk Greek yoghurt and protein powder together with a fork or small whisk until glossy and airy.
2
Warm Compote: Microwave frozen berries in a small dish for 45–60 seconds until juicy, soft, and warm.
3
Assemble Bowls: Spoon whipped yoghurt into bowls, ladle warm berry compote over the top, and drizzle with peanut/almond butter.
4
Garnish & Serve: Scatter chia seeds over the top for crunch and omega-3s. Serve immediately or store covered in fridge up to 24 hrs.
