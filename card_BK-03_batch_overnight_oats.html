Shopping List

  • Rolled Oats & Milk (Cow or Soy)
  • Unflavoured Protein Powder
  • Chia Seeds & Milled Seeds
  • Honey & Vanilla Extract
  • Crunchy Peanut Butter
  • Greek Yoghurt & Mixed Seeds

Preparation Instructions

1
Mix Dry Base: In a large mixing bowl or container, stir together the rolled oats, unflavoured protein powder, chia seeds, and milled seeds until evenly distributed.
2
Whisk Liquids: Pour in the milk (cow or soy), vanilla extract, and honey. Stir thoroughly with a whisk or large spoon to ensure no protein pockets remain.
3
Chill Overnight: Cover the container with a lid or portion directly into 8 meal-prep jars. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (ideally overnight) to thicken.
4
Morning Assembly: Scoop out 1 portion. Top with 1 tsp crunchy peanut butter, 2 tbsp thick Greek yoghurt, a sprinkle of mixed seeds, and fresh berries if desired.
