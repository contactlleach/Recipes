Smoky Mexican Black Bean & Salsa Skillet

Shopping List

Cooking Instructions

1
Sauté Aromatics: Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Sauté diced onion for 3–4 mins until soft and translucent.
2
Build Base: Add drained black beans, sweetcorn, tomato salsa, tomato paste, and 50ml water/stock per 2 portions.
3
Simmer & Thicken: Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer gently for 5–7 mins until liquid thickens into a rich skillet sauce.
4
Plate & Garnish: Spoon into bowls, top with a dollop of Greek yoghurt as a sour cream swap, and sprinkle with toasted seeds/hazelnuts.
