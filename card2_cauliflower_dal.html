Cooking Instructions

1
Roast Cauliflower: Toss florets with oil and half the spice mix. Roast at 200°C for 20 mins until charred and tender.
2
Caramelise Aromatics: Fry sliced onion in oil for 6–8 mins until golden. Add garlic, tomato purée, and remaining spice mix for 1 min.
3
Simmer Dal: Stir in drained lentils, coconut milk, veg stock paste, and 50ml water per 2 portions. Simmer gently for 8–10 mins until rich and creamy.
4
Finish & Plate: Stir in baby spinach and fresh lime juice until wilted. Ladle dal into bowls and top with roasted cauliflower.
