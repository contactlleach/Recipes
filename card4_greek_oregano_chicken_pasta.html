Quick Greek Style Oregano Chicken Pasta Salad

Cooking Instructions

1
Cook Pasta: Cook pasta in boiling salted water for 9–11 mins until al dente. Drain, rinse under cold running water to cool completely, and set aside.
2
Cook Chicken: Pan-fry diced chicken with olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper over high heat for 6–8 mins. Stir through sun-dried tomato paste for the final minute.
3
Whisk Dressing: In a small bowl, combine Greek yoghurt, dried mint, lemon juice, and grated garlic with a pinch of salt.
4
Toss & Serve: Combine cooled pasta, baby leaf mix, sliced red onion, and cucumber. Top with warm oregano chicken and dollop with mint yoghurt.
