Crispy Butter Bean, Pitta Chip & Pear Traybake

Shopping List

  • Butter Beans (drained) & Pitta Breads
  • Fresh Pears, Bell Pepper & Tomato
  • Red Onion & Wild Rocket
  • Amarillo Chilli Paste & Red Wine Vinegar
  • Central American Style Spice Mix
  • Soured Cream

Cooking Instructions

1
Roast Beans & Veg: Preheat oven to 200°C. Toss drained butter beans, sliced bell pepper, diced tomato, and pear wedges on a large lined tray with oil and Central American spice. Roast for 15 mins.
2
Bake Pitta Chips: Cut pitta breads into triangles, toss with oil, salt, and pepper. Add to the tray (or a second tray) for the final 7–8 mins until crisp and browned.
3
Glaze & Quick Pickle: Thinly slice red onion and toss with red wine vinegar and a pinch of salt to pickle. In a ramekin, mix amarillo chilli paste with 1 tsp olive oil and a splash of water for the glaze.
4
Assemble: Transfer roasted beans, pears, and crunchy pitta chips to shallow serving bowls. Drizzle with amarillo glaze, dollop soured cream, and top with pickled onions and fresh wild rocket.
