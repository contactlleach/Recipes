Shopping List

  • Quinoa & Chestnut Mushrooms
  • Fresh Ginger, Garlic & Spring Onions
  • Red Chilli & Fresh Limes
  • Soy Sauce (or Tamari) & Sesame Oil

Cooking Instructions

1
Cook Quinoa: Simmer rinsed quinoa covered in water for 12–15 mins until dry and tender. Season and keep warm.
2
Fry Mushrooms: Stir-fry sliced mushrooms in hot oil for 3–4 mins until browned. Stir in garlic and grated ginger for the final minute.
3
Combine Sauce: Lower heat, toss in spring onions, soy sauce, lime juice, and chilli for 1 min. Stir directly into the cooked quinoa with sesame oil.
4
Crisp Tofu: Dust tofu in cornflour and soy sauce; fry in hot oil until crisp and golden. Serve over the warm quinoa with lime wedges.
