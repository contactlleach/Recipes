Cooking Instructions

1
Bake Chicken: Season chicken with paprika, oregano, oil, salt, and pepper. Bake at 220°C (200°C fan) for 20 mins. Rest 10 mins, then slice.
2
Cook Quinoa: Simmer quinoa in chicken stock for 12–15 mins. Stir in garlic powder and thyme in the last minute. Remove from heat and stir in 1 tbsp Parmesan.
3
Boil Greens: Boil broccoli and kale for 4 mins. Drain, steam dry for 1 min, and toss with olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
4
Finish & Plate: Gently melt butter with crushed garlic for 2–3 mins. Stir 2 tbsp into quinoa; drizzle remainder over chicken and greens.
